- 2026-08-03 | decision | Use a content-first, calm and grounded visual posture; avoid AI-toy aesthetics, gradients, mascots, streaks, confetti and gamification.

- 2026-08-03 | decision | Transcript fallback UX recommends one action at a time and hides provider terminology.
- 2026-08-03 | decision | Tab-audio capture requires an explanation before the browser picker and explicit copy that video is not stored and temporary audio is deleted.

- 2026-08-03 | decision | Generated examples are visually and verbally distinguished from source quotes.
- 2026-08-03 | decision | Open production tasks use self-check criteria; MVP does not fake AI grading of speaking/writing.
- 2026-08-03 | decision | Library rows recover processing/failed jobs, open ready lessons and delete dependent transcript data after confirmation.

- 2026-08-03 | constraint | Browser capture permission opens only from direct user action.
